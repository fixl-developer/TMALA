📏 TEXT PROTECTION RULE

Center 40% horizontal zone = no large card should cross fully.

Cards may slightly overlap edge of text,
but never cover entire word.

This keeps premium readability.

🧭 OVERLAP RULES

Medium overlaps dominant slightly (20px)

Small overlaps medium slightly (10px)

Never overlap all three in same stack

Layering must feel staggered.

🎯 EDGE OVERFLOW RULE

At least one card per panel should:

Overflow container edge by 5–10%

Why?

This removes “boxed” feeling.
Makes it feel editorial & high-end.
